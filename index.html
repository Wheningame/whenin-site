Privacy Policy — «When in …»

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Contact: wheningame@gmail.com.

Short version: the game works offline, has no accounts and no ads, and does not collect personal data.

What stays on your device

Your traveller profile (food allergies, diet, sun and dust sensitivity, motion sickness), the situations you have met, your difficulty choice and your purchases are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

What we receive

Only when you tap the ⚑ report button or send a suggestion: the text you write, the id of the scene, the kind of problem, app version and language. A report never contains your profile, game state, device id, name or e-mail. Reports are collected with Google Forms (Google LLC) and read by the developer to correct content. Please do not put personal data in the text.

Purchases

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store (and the purchase service we use to check what you own, if any). We do not see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Not official advice

The game is educational entertainment, not an official travel guide. For laws, health and entry rules use official government sources.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here. Questions: wheningame@gmail.com.
